Brief biography

I'm a Software Engineering student at Miva Open University in Port Harcourt, Nigeria. Since 2025 I have been a Product Manager at Solagle, where I own the roadmap, write product requirements and prioritise features using customer feedback and usage data.

I also founded EasiBoost Mobility, which converts petrol tricycles (keke) to electric and builds a solar battery-swap network. After interviewing 127 keke drivers, I found that 72% would switch to electric. That research shaped the business model.
